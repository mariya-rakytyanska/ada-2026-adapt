Introduction

Abstract

During our project, we would like to focus on how belonging to a geographical region and historical period influences the links between articles in the WikiSpeedia database. In order to expand our research we will also classify the available articles based on what category they are part of (ex. science, people, geography), thus having one more tool for our analysis.
We would like to answer how articles from different regions are interconnected, specifically asking how the relevance of these links changes across time and how frequently certain navigation paths are taken. Moreover, we want to explore the relationship between article categories and their specific geographical and temporal presence.
Furthermore, to understand how spatial scale alters our perspective and findings, we aim to examine how these dynamics change across three distinct levels of geographical precision: countries, regions, and continents.

Research questions

The main question we would like to pose is the following : How do region, historical period and category affect the standing and links of Wikipedia articles?
Going off of this broad question we will narrow it down to some smaller queries, revealing the many axes of our research : How are countries' articles linked to other regions and are these ties used? How does cultural relevance of countries and their regions evolve over time? Are some categories more relevant in a certain region or time span?
